title: My top five coding books of all time

1. The Pragmatic Programmer,  Andrew Hunt & David Thomas: Makes you want to scream YES! and hand out high fives.
2. The Art of Unix Programming, Eric S Raymond: Excellent book on coding in general and Unix in particular. Applicable even if you never go near Unix internals.
3. Unix Power Tools: Amazing tome with hundreds of byte sized articles on tackling problems on all kinds of Unix systems, including macOS. Some articles are outdated, but most still apply.
4. Python Essential Reference, David Beazley: The book that made me fall in love with Python.
5. Effective Java, Joshua Bloch: Excellent on object oriented challenges in general and Java in particular. Stays lovely core Java, no frameworks.
